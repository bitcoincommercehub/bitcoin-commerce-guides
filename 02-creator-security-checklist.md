# Guide 02: Self-Custody & Security Checklist for Digital Creators

When you accept direct peer-to-peer Bitcoin payments without third-party processors, you become your own bank. Follow this essential checklist to protect your funds and prevent permanent loss.

## 1. Seed Phrase Protection (Critical)
Your 12- or 24-word recovery phrase is the master key to your funds. Anyone who sees it can empty your wallet remotely.
- [ ] **Write it offline:** Record your recovery words on paper or stamped metal immediately upon wallet creation.
- [ ] **Zero digital copies:** Never take a screenshot, never save it in cloud notes (Google Drive, iCloud, Notion), and never send it via email or messaging apps.
- [ ] **Verify your backup:** Before receiving large payments, test restoring your wallet using the written words on a secondary device or clean wallet instance.

## 2. Operational Wallet Separation
Do not keep long-term savings in the same wallet you use for daily website checkouts.
- [ ] **Hot / Receiving Wallet:** Use a mobile or browser Lightning/on-chain wallet for day-to-day customer tips and small digital product sales.
- [ ] **Cold / Savings Storage:** Periodically sweep accumulated funds from your daily receiving wallet to an offline hardware wallet or dedicated cold storage address.

## 3. Safe Payment Verification Habits
- [ ] **Never trust screenshots alone:** Always verify that an on-chain transaction has at least 1–3 confirmations in your own wallet app before delivering high-value software licenses or files.
- [ ] **Double-check address prefixes:** Ensure your public checkout page clearly displays `bc1q...` (Native SegWit) for on-chain BTC and your exact `name@domain.com` format for Lightning.
- [ ] **Beware of phishing:** Bookmark official wallet websites and never enter your 12/24 words into any web form claiming to "verify" or "sync" your wallet.
